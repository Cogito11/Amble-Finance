HOW AMBLE WORKS

The core of Amble.

Amble is meant to be a hub for managing all of your finances in one place. In a world where there's already so much to keep track of, knowing exactly what's going on with your money should be as simple as possible. Amble gives you a single place to log everything your money is doing and lets you see exactly what that means for you.

Start by adding your accounts and creating a budget made up of spending categories you decide on. Then you simply add transactions as they happen, assigning each one to a category as you go. Amble takes that information and turns it into clear visual graphs on your dashboard, so it's easy to see how your spending breaks down by category and make adjustments based on that.

Account

Any place your money lives. Checking accounts, savings accounts, credit cards, and even cash in your wallet. Amble lets you keep tabs on them all so nothing is ever out of view.

Category

A bucket you spend from. Groceries, rent, gas, you decide what the categories are based on how you spend. Every transaction belongs to one, so every dollar is accounted for.

Budget

A spending limit you set for categories. Each area of your spending has a clear number tied to it. Amble shows a live gauge for every budget you set, so you know what's left before you overspend, not after.

Transaction

Anything that moves money. A purchase, a paycheck, a transfer between your own accounts. Logging these is what keeps everything else accurate, so nothing your money is doing slips past you.

WHY IT MATTERS

Money you can't see is money you can't manage.

Most people don't overspend on purpose. They overspend because it's easy to lose track of where your money's going when you don't see the total until it's too late. Having a clear, current picture of your accounts and budget as you spend is one of the most impactful changes you can make in your financial life, and with the right tool, one of the easiest. Here's how Amble helps you see your money clearly in real time.

01

Log every transaction

Amble lets you assign every purchase to a category, so the small charges that are easy to write off in your head and lose track of individually become part of a total that counts towards a budgeted amount.

02

Catch problems while they're small

Amble's graphs and visuals show how much of each category you've used against a given budget, so a category that's three-quarters spent only a week into the month is a warning you can act on before it's too late.

03

Turn goals into numbers

"Save more" isn't a plan that's easy to act on. "Put $200 into savings every payday" is, and Amble makes it easy to stick to your goals by turning them into measurable actions you build into your budget and can see when they're met.

04

Make debt visible, not abstract

Amble gives you a clear visualization of your income over a given timeframe, so it's easy to know exactly where you have to stop spending to stay out of debt.

You don't need a finance background to manage money well. You need to be able to see it clearly, consistently, in one place.

Accounts, all in one place

Track checking, savings, and credit cards together, with your insights calculated automatically as you go.

Budgets you can actually see

Plans made just for you

GETTING STARTED

Up and running in four steps.

With Amble it's easy to get started tracking your finances, the way it should be.

01

Add your accounts

Checking, savings, credit cards, loans, or cash. Enter a starting balance for each and you're already tracking your net worth.

02

Build a budget

Split a paycheck or a monthly income into categories with real limits, and set it as your active plan.

03

Log what you spend

Every expense, income, and transfer is added as a transaction and categorized as you go with everything searchable and filterable later.

04

Watch your gauges

Check the dashboard whenever you like and see exactly what's left in each category, updated in real time.

QUESTIONS

Good to know before you download.

Amble doesn't sync across devices automatically since your data stays local rather than living in the cloud. You can move it yourself by exporting a JSON backup on one device and importing it on another.

As long as you've exported a backup from the Data tab first, you can import that JSON file into a fresh install and pick up right where you left off. Without a backup, uninstalling removes your local data, so it's worth exporting one regularly.

Amble runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux. Builds for Windows and Linux are available below. macOS builds aren't being distributed at the moment (see the download section for why); macOS users can build from source in the meantime.
